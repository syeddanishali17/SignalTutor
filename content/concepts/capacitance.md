**Definition.** Put a voltage $V$ across the plates and a charge $Q$ is stored:

$$Q = C\,V$$

The proportionality constant $C$ is the **capacitance**, unit farad ($1\,\text{F} = 1\,\text{C/V}$). Sensor capacitances are tiny: picofarads, $1\,\text{pF} = 10^{-12}\,\text{F}$.

**Parallel plates.**

$$C = \frac{\varepsilon\,A}{\text{gap}}$$

* numerator $\varepsilon A$: more **area** → more room for charge → larger $C$;
* denominator gap: plates **closer** together → stronger attraction holds more charge → larger $C$;
* $\varepsilon = \varepsilon_r\,\varepsilon_0$ is the permittivity of what fills the gap ($\varepsilon_0 = 8.85\times10^{-12}\,\text{F/m}$; air $\varepsilon_r \approx 1$).

In the sensor the gap is $d - x$: the membrane's deflection $x$ shrinks the gap, so $C$ grows — that is how pressure becomes a capacitance.
