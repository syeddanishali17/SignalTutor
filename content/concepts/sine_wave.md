$$v(t) = A\,\sin(2\pi f\,t)$$

* **Amplitude** $A$: the peak height (in volts for a voltage).
* **Frequency** $f$: how many full cycles per second, in hertz (Hz). One cycle takes the **period** $T = 1/f$.
* **Angular frequency** $\omega = 2\pi f$ (rad/s) — the same thing written for the maths; you will meet it in the capacitive sensor lessons.

**Why 50 Hz matters.** The mains electricity in Europe alternates at **50 Hz**. Every mains cable radiates a little of it, and long sensor wires pick it up like antennas — the most common **interference** in a lab.
