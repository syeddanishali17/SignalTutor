A **force** $F$ (unit newton, N) is a push or a pull. **Pressure** is that push shared out over the area it acts on:

$$p = \frac{F}{A} \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad F = p\,A$$

* numerator $F$: the total push; denominator $A$: the area it is spread over.
* The same force on a **smaller** area gives a **larger** pressure (a needle vs. a thumb).

**Sizes worth remembering**

| situation | pressure |
|---|---|
| air around us | $\approx 100\,\text{kPa}$ |
| weather change in a day | $\approx 1\,\text{kPa}$ |
| loud speech at 1 m | $\approx 0.1$ to $1\,\text{Pa}$ |

On a sensor membrane of $1\,\text{mm}^2 = 10^{-6}\,\text{m}^2$, a pressure difference of $1\,\text{kPa}$ pushes with $F = pA = 10^{3} \times 10^{-6}\,\text{N} = 1\,\text{mN}$.
