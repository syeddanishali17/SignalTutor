Two resistors one after the other, with no junction in between, are **in series**.

* One path ⇒ the **same current** $I$ through both.
* The total opposition adds up: $R_\text{total} = R_1 + R_2$, so

$$I = \frac{V_\text{in}}{R_1 + R_2}$$

* Each resistor takes its share of the supply, $V_{R1} = I\,R_1$ and $V_{R2} = I\,R_2$, and the shares add back up to the supply: $V_{R1} + V_{R2} = V_\text{in}$.

The bigger resistor takes the bigger share of the voltage. That observation *is* the voltage divider.
