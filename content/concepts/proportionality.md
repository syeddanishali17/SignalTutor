If $y = k\,x$ then $y$ is **proportional** to $x$: double $x$ and $y$ doubles. On a graph it is a straight line through the origin; $k$ is its **slope**.

In sensors the slope is called the **sensitivity** or **transfer function** $H$: "how many volts out per unit of input", e.g. volts per pascal.

$$H = \frac{\Delta(\text{output})}{\Delta(\text{input})}$$

Ohm's law is the first example: $I = \frac{1}{R}\,V$. Current is proportional to voltage with slope $1/R$.
