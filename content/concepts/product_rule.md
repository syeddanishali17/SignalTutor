Picture $Q = V\,C$ as the **area of a rectangle** with height $V$ and width $C$.

* If the height grows by $\Delta V$, a strip of area $C\,\Delta V$ is added on top.
* If the width grows by $\Delta C$, a strip $V\,\Delta C$ is added at the side.
* The tiny corner $\Delta V\,\Delta C$ is a change times a change — negligible.

Divide by the time $\Delta t$ it took:

$$\frac{dQ}{dt} = C\,\frac{dV}{dt} + V\,\frac{dC}{dt}$$

This is the capacitor current used here. The two strips are the **two ways** to get a current: change the voltage (barometer), or change the capacitor (microphone).
